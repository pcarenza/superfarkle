Community Rules

Super Farkle · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Super Farkle is a friendly dice game, and players can talk to each other by text and by voice. By playing you agree to follow these rules.

Be decent

  • No harassment, bullying, threats or hate speech, in chat, in voice or in your display name.
  • No sexual or graphic content, and nothing that targets or endangers minors.
  • Don't share anyone's personal information, including your own address, phone number or school.
  • No spam, scams or advertising.
  • No cheating, and no tampering with the game or its servers.

Your tools

  • Block a player to hide their chat and mute their voice. Tap their name in the chat, the voice list or the lobby, then choose Block. Blocks are remembered on your device, and you can undo one from the Blocked list in the chat.
  • Report a player the same way, choosing Report this player. A report records the reason, an optional note and the last few chat messages in the room so it can be reviewed.
  • Leave any room at any time, and mute yourself or others in voice chat.

What we may do

We review reports and may remove players or rooms that break these rules, and may block access to the game. We can't watch every room live, and voice chat is not recorded, so please use the block and report tools right away if someone makes you uncomfortable.

Young players

The game is for players 13 and over. Younger players should not use it without a parent's supervision, and only with people they know.

The fine print

The game is provided free and as is, without warranties, and it may change or stop at any time. Games are held in memory only and can end if the server restarts.

Contact

Questions, appeals or urgent concerns: open an issue on the project page. Please don't include personal information in a public issue.
