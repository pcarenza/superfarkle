Delete Your Data

Super Farkle (web game and Android app) · Last updated: November 25, 2026

Super Farkle has no accounts, so there is no profile to delete. The game keeps very little about you, and most of it is removed automatically. Here is what exists and how to remove it.

Data on our server: removed automatically

Your display name, chat messages, game activity, color and a random reconnect identifier are held in the game server's memory only, not in a database. They are deleted when:

  • you tap ✕ to leave the game (your seat and name are removed from the room), and
  • the room ends: it is deleted when everyone has left, or after about 30 minutes with nobody connected, and whenever the server restarts.

Voice chat is never recorded or stored.

Data on your device: you can clear it at any time

The game saves your name, settings, block list and reconnect identifier on your device only.

  • Android app: Settings → Apps → Super Farkle → Storage → Clear data (or uninstall the app).
  • Website: clear the site data for superfarkle.onrender.com in your browser's settings.

Reports in the server log

If someone reported you, or you sent a report, a record is kept in the server's log: the reason, an optional note, the last few chat messages in the room, and the names and anonymous IDs involved. We can't edit individual log lines, and these records are removed when our hosting provider's log retention period ends.

Request deletion or ask a question

If you want us to look at something specific, such as a chat message that should be removed from the logs sooner, open an issue on the project page. Say roughly when you played and your display name, but do not include any other personal information, because issues are public. We'll respond there and remove what we can.
