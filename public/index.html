      How to play

      1. Goal: be the first to bank the target score (10,000 or 5,000). When someone gets there, everyone else gets one last turn to beat them.
      2. Your turn: roll all six dice. Tap the dice that score to keep them, then either roll the rest or bank your points.
      3. Farkle! If a roll has no scoring dice, you lose everything you scored this turn.
      4. Hot dice: score with all six dice and you get to roll all six again.
      5. Getting on the board: your first banked turn must total at least 500.
      6. Every die you keep must score.
