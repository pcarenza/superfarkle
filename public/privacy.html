Privacy Policy

Super Farkle (web game and Android app) · Last updated: November 24, 2026 (voice chat added)

Super Farkle is a free multiplayer dice game. It has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics. This policy explains what the game handles when you play.

We do not ask for, and the game does not collect, your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or camera. The Android app requests internet access, and microphone access only when you choose to use voice chat (see below). It also uses your phone's vibration (for turn alerts) and a screen-awake setting during games, neither of which collects data.

Voice chat (optional)

Voice chat is off until you tap Join voice. Only then does the game ask for permission to use your microphone, and you can mute or leave at any time. Your voice is sent directly from your device to the other players who joined voice chat in the same game, and it is encrypted in transit. We do not record it, store it or listen to it. Our server only passes along the messages that set up each connection.

Because the connections are direct, the other players in the voice chat may be able to learn your device's network (IP) address. Devices find each other with the help of a public STUN server (currently run by Google), which sees your IP address when you join. If a direct connection is not possible, your encrypted audio may be relayed through a relay (TURN) server run by a third-party provider (currently Cloudflare), which sees your IP address while relaying.

How long it is kept

Games are kept in the server's memory only. They are not stored in a database. A game room is deleted when everyone has left, or after about 30 minutes with nobody connected. Restarting or updating the server also clears all games. Anything saved on your device stays there until you clear the app's data or your browser's site data.

Sharing

Your display name, color, chat messages and game activity are visible to the other people in the same game, including anyone who joins with the room code or invite link. Don't type personal information into your name or the chat. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Hosting

The game server runs on Render. Like most web hosts, Render may process technical information such as your IP address in its connection logs when you connect. We don't use that information to identify you. See Render's own privacy policy for how it handles it. If you install the app through Google Play, Google handles the installation under its own policies.

Children

Super Farkle is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because players can chat, and talk by voice, with each other, young children should only play with people they know and with a parent's supervision.

Your choices

  • Leave a game at any time using the ✕ button. Your chat and game data is then removed from the server when the room ends.
  • Clear the saved name and settings by clearing the app's data (Android) or the site data (browser).
